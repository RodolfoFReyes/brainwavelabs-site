# PM Console Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 6, 2026*

PM Console is an app made by Brainwave Labs LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it, on any device it runs on. The short version: **we do not collect any of your data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. PM Console has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting of its own. We have no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see anything you enter into the app.

## Where your data is stored

Your projects, budgets, people, and other information are saved on your device, in the app's data folder (or a folder you choose in Settings). Backups you make, and the automatic daily backups, are saved on your device or wherever you choose to save them. The app's activity log, which records changes made in the app, is stored in the same data folder. You can delete any of this at any time by deleting those files or the app.

## Optional features that send data

Two optional features send information over the internet. Neither is turned on unless you set it up, and neither runs unless you choose an action that uses it. In both cases the information goes directly from your device to that company, under your own account with them. It does not pass through us, and we never receive it.

**AI drafting (Anthropic).** If you enter your own Anthropic API key in Settings, you can ask the app to draft a funding-request memo, rewrite a section of it, proofread it, or write an executive summary of your portfolio. When you choose one of those actions, the text the feature needs is sent to Anthropic's API: for a memo, your answers to its questionnaire; for an executive summary, a summary of your projects, budgets, people's allocations, milestones, and risks. While a key is set, the app also asks Anthropic about once a day which Claude models are available; that request sends only your key. Anthropic's handling of this data is covered by your agreement with Anthropic and its privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

**Jira link.** If you enter your own Jira site address, email, and API token in Settings and link a roadmap initiative to a Jira board, choosing "Refresh" asks your Jira site for that board's current sprint. Only the board's ID is sent; sprint names, dates, and point totals come back and are saved in your data folder. This is covered by your agreement with Atlassian and its privacy policy: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Your keys and passwords

Your Anthropic API key and Jira API token are stored encrypted in your device's secure storage (on Apple devices, the Keychain). They are only ever sent to Anthropic or your Jira site, respectively, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

## App stores

You get PM Console through an app store, such as Apple's App Store, which handles your purchase and download under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, the store may share anonymous usage and crash information with us. We use it only to fix problems.

## Children

PM Console is a business tool and is not directed at children.
