PM Console

Your roadmap, budget, and team in one app.

Plan initiatives, ask for funding, track every dollar, and see who is working on what, without juggling a stack of spreadsheets.

Coming soon to the Mac App StoreFor Mac, macOS 13 or newer

Roadmap

See the whole roadmap at a glance

Lay out initiatives by quarter, break them into features with their own dates, and track milestones and risks. A dashed line shows where today falls. Export the timeline to Excel, ready to paste into a slide.

Funding requests

Make a stronger case for the money

A guided questionnaire walks you through the problem, the options, and the costs. PM Console estimates payback, IRR, and NPV as you type, then builds a memo and a financial workbook. AI drafting is there if you want it, never required.

Finances

Know where every dollar stands

Budget, forecast, and actual spending for every project, month by month. Burn charts show where you are headed, and you can paste actuals straight from a spreadsheet or import a template.

People and capacity

Spot overload before it happens

See who is working on what, month by month. Click any month to rebalance someone's time on the spot, and see what each person and department costs.

Backlog

Keep day-to-day work moving

A flexible board for the smaller tasks: your own columns, groups, and statuses, in a table or as cards you drag between lanes.

Private by design

No account

Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.

No tracking

No analytics, ads, or crash reports. We can't see what you do in the app.

Stays on your device

Your plans, budgets, and people are saved on your own computer, with backups you control.

Two optional features, AI drafting and Jira, send data only when you set them up and use them, straight to those services under your own account. Read the privacy policy.
